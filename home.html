Our History

About Our College

St. Andrew's College Ssanda (SACS) was founded in 2009 by Msgr. Dr. Lawrence L. Ssemusu at Ssanda Village, Kajjansi Town Council within Wakiso District. The institution was built on a vision to bridge the gap between abstract academic theory and practical innovation.

We serve as a premier co-educational (mixed) Day and Boarding secondary school, cultivating a vibrant environment where learning, creativity, faith, and lifelong friendships intertwine.

Admissions & Guidelines

We welcome students from diverse backgrounds who are determined to learn, grow, and leverage their talents.

O-Level Entry Requirements

  • Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) result slip and certificate.
  • Birth Certificate (original copy alongside a clear photocopy for validation).
  • Most recent progressive school report cards from P7.
  • Four (4) identical student passport-size photographs.

A-Level Entry Requirements

  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) results slip or official certificate.
  • Valid Birth Certificate.
  • Academic reference or recommendation letter from the previous headteacher.
  • Four (4) clean passport-size photographs.

Admission Procedure Breakdown

  1. Step 1: Application Form: Collect an application form directly from the school administrative office.
  2. Step 2: Submission: Return the compiled physical form coupled with all the mandatory structural documents listed.
  3. Step 3: Evaluation: Short listed candidates may be invited for a brief interview assessment to evaluate academic readiness.
